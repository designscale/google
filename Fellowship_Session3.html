Session 2 — Building Intelligence Architectures 🔜

August 5, 2026

Theme

How does FIT's CPA model become globally scalable?

Carried forward from Session 1

Google's REVEAL framework and "Grounded Reasoning" gave CPA a citable academic precedent (see the Session 1 recap) and sharper vocabulary — Attribution Error vs. Logical Error, "Thinking Design" over "AI Policing." Worth carrying that vocabulary into the AI Pedagogy Toolkit so it reads as aligned with Google's own research, not just FIT's internal framing.

What to prepare (after Session 1)

  • Define the AI Pedagogy Toolkit — what's in it, who's it for
  • Map adoption pathway: FIT → SUNY → global design programs
  • Identify Google for Education partnership opportunities
  • Draft open-source distribution model
  • Frame Pomelli + expanded Nano Banana use as Phase 2

Vision: The AI Pedagogy Toolkit (Open Source)

  • CPA rubric templates (adaptable across design disciplines)
  • Google Stitch workflow example for UX/UI educators
  • Gemini Notebook research-to-wireframe example
  • Empowering Design Education with AI (sabbatical book) — the pedagogical foundation behind the toolkit
